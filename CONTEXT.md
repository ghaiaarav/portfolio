# Portfolio Glossary

- **World**: A portfolio project shown in the Singleplayer list.
- **Server**: A professional or research experience shown in the Multiplayer list.
- **Resource**: A downloadable or externally viewable artifact such as a resume, paper, or project.
- **Media Case Study**: Photos and videos associated with one Server.
- **Discovery**: One of five unique hidden interactions. Discoveries persist on the visitor's device.
- **Secret Room**: The gallery and credits museum unlocked after all five Discoveries.
