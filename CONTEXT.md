# Portfolio

Minecraft-themed personal portfolio for Aarav Ghai. The menu language is the product language.

## Language

**World**:
A portfolio project shown in the Singleplayer list.
_Avoid_: Project card, case study

**Server**:
A professional or research experience shown in the Multiplayer list.
_Avoid_: Job, role card

**Media Case Study**:
Photos or videos filtered to one Server.
_Avoid_: Gallery tab, album

**Resource**:
A downloadable or externally readable artifact such as a resume, paper, or project.
_Avoid_: Skill, honor, resume bullet

**Discovery**:
One unique easter egg found by a visitor.
_Avoid_: Cheat, secret click

**Secret Room**:
The chest museum unlocked after all Discoveries are found.
_Avoid_: Credits page, hidden gallery

**Origin**:
The trusted menu that opened a shared screen and receives the visitor when they select Done.
_Avoid_: Referrer, back stack
